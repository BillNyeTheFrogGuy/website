Bio

Daniel Lazzaretto is a Southern California musician and audio engineer, currently completing a B.A. in Music from UC Irvine. Primarily a jazz saxophone performer, his main influence is the bop of the late 50s and early 60s, taking inspiration from artists such as Cannonball Adderley, Sonny Stitt, and Hank Mobley.
