Original Compositions

Arrival
This is a little demo I put together in the summer of 2026 for a potential song on my senior recital the following spring. The music is inspired by the bossa nova stylings of Jobim and the like, as well as the children's book series "I Spy", specifically "I Spy Treasure Hunt".
Moon Zones (Studio Version)
This is something I composed in a jazz composition course taught by Sasha Berliner. Initially performed at the end-of-quarter concert, this is another take from a studio session I put together for my classmates and I to have our compositions on record (see below).
Don't Forget the Funk
Here's my first composition ever heard by an audience. The members of my combo that quarter all brought in originals, so we decided to make it an all-originals set. I had been mulling some of these ideas over for a while and came up with this tune.

Combo Secording Session
